In-Context Sharpness as Alerts: An Inner Representation Perspective for Hallucination Mitigation discovers that correct generations tend to have sharper context activations in the hidden states of the in-context tokens. Leveraging this signal, an entropy-based metric quantifies the sharpness and is incorporated into the decoding process to improve factuality and generated quality.
