On the Universal Truthfulness Hyperplane Inside LLMs examines whether a universal truthfulness hyperplane exists inside the model, by designing and training a probe on diverse datasets. The approach greatly improves existing results and conveys positive signals on the existence of such a universal truthfulness hyperplane.
